SuperSonic Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

SuperSonic does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

What the app does not do
- It does not ask for any permissions, including internet access.
- It has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics or crash reporting.
- It does not send anything to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device
- The code you write, which is kept in the app's private storage.
- The sounds the app unpacks when it first starts.
This data never leaves your device through SuperSonic. Uninstalling the app,
or clearing its data, deletes it.

Files you open or save
When you open or save a .rb file, you choose it yourself with Android's
system file picker. SuperSonic can reach only the file you pick, and only at
that moment.

Device backup
If you have turned on Android's backup, Android may include SuperSonic's data
(such as your code) in your device backup with Google. This is controlled by
your device settings and Google's own privacy policy, not by SuperSonic.

Children
SuperSonic collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new
effective date.
